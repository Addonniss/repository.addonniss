1.1.2
- Stinger now works with streaming add-ons (POV, TMDbHelper, etc.) — no longer limited to library movies only
- New: early heads-up notification when starting a movie that has extra scenes during or after the credits
- New settings: choose when the notification appears (0–60 seconds) and how long it stays (1–30 seconds)
- The end-of-playback alert now appears at a better moment — before the credits roll, not during the extra scene itself
- The overlay button now reads "Stay Tuned" instead of "Stinger"

1.1.1
- Added Stinger alerts for movies: detects mid-credits and post-credits scenes using TMDB, AfterCredits.com, and Wikipedia
- Stinger alert appears near the end of movie playback when a post-credits scene is detected; no alert means the user can safely stop
- Stinger uses the last chapter marker for timing, with a configurable percentage fallback (default 85%)
- Stinger is enabled by default with no configuration needed — all databases query automatically under the hood
